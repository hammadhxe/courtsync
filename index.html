The problem

Court work is scattered across diaries, websites and phones

The next date sits in one lawyer's diary. Orders are on a court website nobody checked this week. The cause list is a PDF. A task lives in someone's head. When a date moves, not everyone hears about it.

The fix

One shared record that keeps itself current

  • Dates, orders and cause lists are read from the courts for you.
  • Everyone sees the same hearing lists and tasks.
  • The right alert reaches the right person on their phone.

What you get

Everything a litigation desk checks, every day

The daily work of a chambers, handled in one app without a long setup.

The next date on every case

Each case shows its next court date and its full hearing history. Use Today, This Week, a calendar, or sort by client, court or lawyer. Export any list to Excel.

Dates that update themselves

Every night CourtSync checks your cases against district courts, the High Court, the tribunal (CAT) and consumer commissions, and updates the next date. A "Check now" button is on each case.

Hearing lists on your phone

At 9 AM, today's hearings. At 6 PM, tomorrow and the rest of the week. On Monday at 8 AM, the full week. Real phone notifications, and you choose which ones you want.

Court orders fetched for you

New High Court orders are downloaded to the case and you get an alert. If the court has not uploaded the order yet, CourtSync keeps checking every night until it appears.

Display Board Tracker

Tick your cases for today and CourtSync watches the court's live display board. You get alerts at 3 cases to go, 2 to go, next, and called, naming your case and the judge.

Cause lists, matched to your cases

Official cause lists are read through the day. Your cases are found on them, the date is set, and the list prints as a clean A4 sheet with serial number, bench and advocates.

Tasks for the team

Give a task to any lawyer, for a case or for a client whose case is not filed yet. Set priority and a due date, and get a morning reminder for what is overdue or due soon.

Add cases by name

Add cases by an advocate's name or a party's name, across High Courts, district courts and the tribunal. Or bring in a whole list from the eCourts app export.

Overnight watchlist

Name the clients you represent. Each night CourtSync looks for new cases filed against them in J&K courts and sends the result in the morning. It tells you plainly if any search could not finish.

Questions

Questions lawyers ask before they register

What is CourtSync?

CourtSync is a case management system for litigation firms in India. It keeps each case's next date and history, updates dates from the courts, sends hearing lists and order alerts to your phone, tracks your cases on the live display board, and gives your team tasks.

Who is it for?

Litigation chambers and law firms in India, from a small team to a large one.

Which courts does it cover?

Dates are checked against district courts, the High Court, the Central Administrative Tribunal (CAT) and consumer commissions. Some tools, such as the Display Board Tracker, court order fetching and the High Court cause list, currently work for the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh. Write to us if you need another court.

Do court dates update by themselves?

Yes. Every night CourtSync checks your cases and updates the next date. Cause lists are also read during the day. You can press "Check now" on any case to check it straight away.

What notifications will I get?

Hearing lists at 9 AM and 6 PM, a weekly list on Monday, alerts for new court orders, and live alerts when your case is about to be called. You choose which ones to switch on, per phone.

Does it get court orders for me?

Yes, for High Court cases. New orders are downloaded and saved on the case, and you get an alert. If an order is not uploaded yet, CourtSync keeps checking each night until it is.

What is the Display Board Tracker?

It watches the court's live display board for the cases you tick. You get an alert at 3 cases to go, 2 to go, next, and when your case is called, so you can be in the right courtroom in time.

How do I get started?

Register your firm on the login screen of the app. We approve it and start your free trial. Then you add your cases and your team.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every registered firm gets a free trial once it is approved. We start the trial for you after approval. For anything else about the service, write to hello@court-sync.xyz.

How do I sign in?

With your email. We send you a six digit code, you type it in, and you are in. There are no passwords.

How many lawyers can use it?

As many as your firm needs. There is no limit on the number of lawyers.

Do I need to install anything?

No store download is needed. CourtSync is a web app, and you can add it to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.

Can my firm use its own name and logo?

Yes. Each firm gets its own address, name, colours and logo.

Where it came from

Built in a working chambers, then opened to others

CourtSync was first built for Malik & Muazzam Law Chambers in Srinagar, to keep its own cases and hearing dates in order. It is now offered to other litigation firms.
