The problem

Dates live in diaries, phones and memory

A litigation firm tracks hundreds of cases. The next date sits in one lawyer's diary, an order is in a WhatsApp group, and a task is in someone's head. When a date moves, not everyone hears about it.

The fix

One shared record for the whole firm

  • Each case holds its next date and full history.
  • Everyone sees the same daily and weekly hearing list.
  • Tasks and alerts reach the right person on their phone.

Questions

Questions lawyers ask before they register

What is CourtSync?

CourtSync is a case management system for litigation firms in India. It keeps each case's next court date and history, daily and weekly hearing lists, the court's cause lists, tasks for your team and phone notifications.

Who is it for?

Litigation chambers and law firms in India, from a small team to a large one. It was first built for Malik & Muazzam Law Chambers in Srinagar and is now offered to other firms.

How do I get started?

Register your firm on the login screen of the app. We approve it and start your free trial. Then you add your cases and your team.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every registered firm gets a free trial once it is approved. We start the trial for you after approval. Write to us if you want to know more.

How do I sign in?

With your email. We send you a six digit code, you type it in, and you are in. There are no passwords.

How many lawyers can use it?

As many as your firm needs. There is no limit on the number of lawyers.

Do I need to install anything?

No store download is needed. CourtSync is a web app, and you can add it to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.

Can my firm use its own name and logo?

Yes. Each firm gets its own address, name, colours and logo.

How do I add my cases?

You can add cases by advocate name, so you do not have to enter each case by hand.
