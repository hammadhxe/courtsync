Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

CourtSync is a case management app for litigation firms. This page explains in plain words what we keep and why. It covers the CourtSync app and this website.

What we store

CourtSync stores the case details a law firm enters into the app. It also stores the case details the app reads from court websites for that firm's cases.

Each firm's data stays separate

Every firm's data is kept separate from every other firm's. We never sell it and we never share it.

Google Drive, if your firm connects it

A firm can choose to connect its own Google Drive. If it does, CourtSync asks Google for the "drive.file" permission. This means CourtSync can only reach the files it creates itself. It cannot open your other files.

Those files are saved in that firm's own Drive. A firm can disconnect Google Drive at any time. You can also remove CourtSync's access from your Google Account settings.

Email addresses

We use an email address only to send login codes.

This website

This website has no accounts and no forms. It counts visits and clicks without using cookies. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Contact

For any question about privacy, write to hello@court-sync.xyz.
